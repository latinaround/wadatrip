# Pilot commercial decisions

## Marketplace commission

On 2026-10-14, Wadatrip's owner approved a **15% marketplace commission for the pilot**.

For a USD 50 tour booking, the intended split is USD 7.50 for Wadatrip and USD 42.50
for the operator, before any additional costs. This is not a statement of net profit
or the amount ultimately deposited in a bank account.

This approval does not change hosted configuration, enable Stripe Live, establish
operator acceptance, or authorize changing existing bookings or financial history.
The operator agreement must make processor fees, currency conversion, refunds,
disputes and payout costs explicit before real sales.

## Operator legal identity

Recommended marketplace policy: support independent individuals and registered
businesses where their actual legal status, local operating requirements and payment
processor eligibility permit. Do not require incorporation solely to join the pilot.

The payee must represent the actual contracting operator. Do not choose a company
or country merely to bypass onboarding restrictions. Wadatrip's platform legal entity
and an operator's legal identity are separate facts.

For the Kiara pilot, the owner has declared that Kiara will participate personally
and lives in the United States. These are owner declarations, not completed KYC,
tax-residency verification or processor approval. The tour location is Peru.

The owner subsequently confirmed that Kiara herself will operate the tour and
receive payment as an independent guide. This establishes the declared pilot
operator, not independently verified service delivery or legal eligibility. The
requested Peru-resident guide scenario is a Sandbox simulation for the initial
Peru supplier cohort; it does not change Kiara's real residence or legal identity.

Receiving into a Peru bank account is the scenario under evaluation. Actual bank
ownership and eligibility have not been established; no bank details were collected.
Do not create a Peru legal identity merely because the tour or bank is in Peru.

The following remain unconfirmed:

- Whether the proposed Peru bank account actually exists and belongs to the payee.
- The appropriate verified legal-country classification and processor funds flow.
- Operational confirmation that the advertised departure can actually be delivered.
- Live eligibility and operator acceptance of commission and cost allocation.

Sandbox onboarding is technical evidence only. It does not establish Live eligibility
or verify the actual operator's legal identity.

## Settlement direction accepted by the platform owner

The owner agreed to proceed with preparation of a simple marketplace model:
retain current card collection, separate operator entitlements from platform income,
and define a weekly settlement cycle for eligible performed services. This direction
is not an executed operator agreement or an implemented payout scheduler.

The specific cutoff, provider-permitted holding period, bank arrival estimate, handling
of claims/refunds, fee allocation and any advance for tickets must be agreed before
real sales. The 24-hour traveler cancellation cutoff does not authorize an operator
payout by itself. Existing destination charges currently transfer to the connected
balance at payment; bank payout timing is a separate operation.

See [the launch packet](PILOT_LAUNCH_PACKET.md) for the pending decisions, prepared
provider questions, settlement proposal and implementation sequence.

## Supplier collaboration: manual validation first — 2026-10-15

The owner accepted a manual-first experiment with Kiara and 1–2 prospective Lima
transport providers, after discussing a future supplier-opportunities inbox and alerts.
Prioritize the first safely delivered real booking; do not build matching, alerts,
automatic assignments or supplier payments yet. Quote requests and expressions of
interest are not confirmed services or guaranteed sales.

Use [the manual supplier pilot](MANUAL_SUPPLIER_PILOT.md) to obtain real quotes,
verify capacity/operating arrangements and record commercial outcomes. No transport
provider, quote, margin or contract has been established by this decision. Existing
Live-payment gates and the approved 15% pilot commission remain unchanged.
