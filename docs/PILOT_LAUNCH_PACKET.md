   explicitly allocated to the operator. Do not deduct unspecified costs silently.

   financial costs separately. Commission is not net profit; operator funds are not
   discretionary platform income. Bookkeeping treatment requires appropriate advice.
3. Proposed weekly cutoff: Sunday 23:59 America/Lima for performed eligible services.
   Proposed submission day: Monday, or next applicable banking business day. These
   dates are a draft, not a live promise, pending operator and processor approval.
4. Eligible means service performance is established, payment is verified and available,
   payout identity/route are approved, and no unresolved issue prevents payment under
   the agreed terms. Bank processing follows submission and must be disclosed separately.
5. No automatic pre-service advance in the first pilot. Any necessary ticket advance
   requires a separate documented agreement and authorization; do not disguise it as
   an ordinary completed-service settlement.
6. A claim or failed payout must have a reason, responsible person and next review date.
   Never keep an operator's funds indefinitely without a defined recovery process.
7. A failed payout does not erase the payable balance. Retry only after reconciling the
   prior attempt and correcting its cause. Record refunds/reversals as separate events.
8. Traveler cancellation terms and operator settlement eligibility are different rules.
   A nonrefundable traveler cancellation does not prove the operator performed the tour.
9. Resolve Test holds through the existing booking/payment lifecycle before real inventory
